Who we are

This website, regatta.tech, is operated by Iditarod Bio, Inc. (“Regatta”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal information collected through this site. This policy explains what we collect when you visit regatta.tech or request a pilot, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

It covers the website only. Data processed inside a Regatta installation on a customer's infrastructure is governed by that customer's pilot or service agreement, not by this policy.

How we use it

  • To respond to your pilot request and, if you go ahead, to set up and run the pilot. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To contact you about Regatta, including the pilot you asked about. We do not add you to a newsletter or marketing list without asking you first. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in following up on a request you made; you can object at any time.
  • To operate, protect and improve the website, including detecting abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that act on our instructions:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts this website and stores submissions of the “Request a pilot” form.
  • Our email provider, which delivers and stores our correspondence with you.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

International transfers. We are based in the United States and our providers may process your information there. Where information is transferred from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the provider's equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Pilot requests: for as long as we are in contact with you about a pilot, and up to 24 months after our last exchange if no pilot goes ahead.
  • Correspondence: for as long as we need it to deal with your query and for our records, normally no more than 3 years.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period (typically 30 days) for security and troubleshooting.

We keep information longer only where the law requires it or where it is needed to resolve a dispute.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking. Fonts and media are served from our own domain, so no third-party content is loaded when you visit. If that changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we process it;
  • receive the information you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing that already took place.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us. We will respond within one month, or sooner where the law requires. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to form submissions and email is limited to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the kind of information we hold.

Children

regatta.tech is a business site intended for adults working in clinical development. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change is significant, we will make that clear on this page before it takes effect.
