Decision at stake

15% of first-line NSCLC (KMT2C or KMT2D) and 33% of urothelial cancer. The decision affected is where ICI is optional: PD-L1-negative NSCLC and cisplatin-ineligible urothelial cancer.

Not yet shown

GENIE lung could not estimate it. Second line runs opposite in three datasets: OAK/POPLAR 1.09 (0.73-1.61), GENIE lung 2.78 (0.64-12.13), bladder 1.25 (0.51-3.06). The bladder result is the first comparative estimate outside lung.

Decision at stake

6% of first-line lung adenocarcinoma. If true, it supports ICI-containing first-line therapy in this group. No published estimate found.

Not yet shown

GENIE holds one ICI-treated co-mutant patient. OAK/POPLAR hold 8: RBM10 alone gives survival 0.73 (0.27-2.02) and PFS 0.47 (0.17-1.25). MSK-CHORD PFS interaction 0.82. Candidate mechanism: RBM10 loss changes RNA splicing and could create new protein fragments the immune system recognizes (splice-derived neoantigens).

Sources: Regatta handoff evidence ledger (Oct 28, 2026): MSK-CHORD discovery (round 1 lung screen, 1,164 tests, two-sided p; round 2 reviewed set, 57 tests, one-sided p, direction written down before testing), GENIE BPC replication outside MSK, Bladder 1.0 first-line cross-cancer test on 16 ICI-treated mutant patients; combined = inverse-variance pool of MSK-CHORD lung and bladder. Interaction hazard ratio (HR) for overall survival: below 1, the checkpoint inhibitor does relatively better when the feature is present; 95% CI. Observational data.
