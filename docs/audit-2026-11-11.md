# regatta.tech audit, 11 November 2026

Audit of the marketing site (regatta.tech only, not app.regatta.tech) run by Daniela with Claude. It covered what changed since the move to AWS on 9 Nov, whether the repo matched production, and a UX/UI review of every page on desktop and phone.

## Summary

- The site worked and looked coherent, but the repo was behind production and `main` was not safe to publish.
- The AWS side was fixed and applied the same day.
- The site fixes are in PR #15 and are not published yet.
- Open points for Monica are in issue #14.

## What we found

### Repo and production did not match

| Item | Production (11 Nov, before the audit) | `main` |
|---|---|---|
| `/home/` | Version uploaded on 10 Nov: "Book a demo", new capability copy, new app demo, `manish-sharma-2.jpg` | Older version: "Let's meet", previous demo |
| Form | Posts to `/api/pilot` (AWS) | Still the Netlify form (`action="/thanks/"`) |
| AWS template | Four fixes applied by hand on 9 Nov | None of the four |
| README | n/a | Still described Netlify |
| Everything else | Identical, byte for byte | Identical |

Publishing `site/` from `main` would have replaced the live `/home/` and silently lost every form submission.

### What Monica changed (9 to 10 Nov)

- 9 Nov: straightened Manish's photo (same 320x320 file, rotated). Live as `manish-sharma-2.jpg`.
- 10 Nov, 16:31: uploaded the current `/home/` to S3. Not committed.
- 10 Nov, 22:45: PR #13, cover buttons renamed "Login" and "Product". Published by uploading `index.html` only.

Nothing of Daniela's earlier work was overwritten.

### Form

- Neither field was required (email lost `required` on 2 Nov), so an empty submit went through.
- An invalid email sent the visitor back to `/home/#pilot`, an anchor that does not exist, with no message.
- An organization name with accents or curly quotes made the receiver fail (the text went into the email subject, which must be plain ASCII). The visitor saw an error and the request was lost.
- The notification email included the visitor's IP and browser, while the page says only email and organization are collected.

### Privacy policy

- Named Netlify as host and as the place where submissions are stored.
- Said "contact us" without giving any contact.

### Design and UX

- Section content used two different left edges above 1280 px (40 px apart), and section titles alternated between 44 and 48 px.
- Tablet widths (721 to 1023 px) keep the two-column layout and get cramped: names, bios and headings wrap badly.
- The app demo is a fixed 980x560 stage scaled to the screen. On a 390 px phone the answer text renders at about 5 px.
- The cover's buttons were cut off on landscape phones.
- The phone menu did not close with Esc or by tapping outside.
- `/thanks/` and the 404 page used an orange (#FF5A1F) that is not the brand orange.
- Chrome's autofill painted the email field white on the navy form.
- No security headers were set on AWS (none existed on Netlify either).

### What was fine

- No horizontal overflow from 320 to 1920 px.
- Contrast passes AA almost everywhere.
- No third-party requests, trackers or console errors.
- Every in-page anchor resolves; titles, descriptions, sitemap and robots.txt are in order.
- Reduced-motion is respected (the cover video is not even loaded).

## What was done

### In PR #15 (not published yet)

- `/home/` from production committed unchanged, then fixes on top.
- Form: required fields, messages for an invalid email and for a failed send, navy background kept on autofill.
- All sections and the footer aligned to the header edge; all section titles at 44 px.
- Regatta comparison card: dividers in the light label tone instead of orange.
- Privacy policy: AWS as host and processor, Contact section (Iditarod Bio, Inc., regatta@iditarod.bio), "Book a demo" in the header, effective date 11 Nov 2026.
- `/thanks/` and 404: no orange rule, two borderless links ("Back to Regatta", "Login"), small footer.
- Cover: scales as one piece on landscape phones. Portrait and desktop unchanged.
- Phone menu closes with Esc and on outside tap.
- AWS template, publish script, README and guide (see below).

### Applied on AWS on 11 Nov (already live)

Applied to the `regatta-website` stack with a change set. Five resources changed, none replaced.

- Form receiver: accents no longer break it, failed sends are logged and show a message, no IP or browser in the email, Node.js 24.
- Security headers on every response: HSTS, CSP, nosniff, frame and referrer policies.
- URL function keeps the query string when redirecting `/home` to `/home/`.
- Monica added as third recipient of the form emails (needs to confirm AWS's email once).

Tested live after the update: headers present, pages load with no CSP violations, and a real submission with "Société Générale" as organization arrived by email.
